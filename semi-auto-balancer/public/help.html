1. What this tool is

A semi-automatic rebalancer: it watches your portfolio (one flat pool of assets per profile), and when an asset drifts far enough from its target weight it notifies you with the exact trades to make. Nothing trades itself, and nothing changes settings itself — every analysis is advisory-with-apply. The two analysis tools (tuner and lab) are backtests over real exchange history (~4 years daily where available), with honesty rails: costs always on, out-of-sample confirmation, and explicit "no edge found" verdicts.

2. Assets grid & the tethered index

  • Index checkbox — exactly one asset is the tethered index (USDC, USD, MXN…). Every value, basket and backtest is denominated in it. Switching it splices the track record continuously — then use Set new targets to give the new tether a real band.
  • Trigger @ — the per-asset drift trigger, derived from the profile sensitivity X by weight-normalization X(1−w)/(1+wX), so a 4% asset and a 56% asset both react at about the same own-price move. The tether itself never trades on its own breach.
  • Status — the buy-freeze badge (click to unfreeze), the standing ignore freeze toggle (visible on every active asset, persists across freeze/unfreeze), and the depeg flag. See Safety rails.

3. Targets, basket & value index

Set new targets opens the editor; targets (index included) must total 100%. Two chained series survive every change:

  • Currency basket — unit growth from trading only. Deposits, withdrawals and target changes splice through it without a jump; only buys/sells (and market moves between them) move it. Basket > 1.0 means rebalancing has accumulated units.
  • Value index — performance in index-currency terms since value started; feeds the annualized (compounding) rate on the profile summary.

Record manual deposits / withdrawals in their section (or let exchange sync detect them) — they are spliced, never counted as gains.

4. Sensitivity tuner (backtest)

  • Basket / Value / vs hold — whole-window results per X; vs hold is the harvest edge over doing nothing. A recommendation appears only where basket AND value agree on a plateau; divergence gives a warning instead.
  • ½ recent / ¼ recent — the same X over the most recent half and quarter of the history, each against its own hold baseline. An X that shines on all-history but bleeds in the recent quarter is an edge that died.
  • Hypothetical sweeps — arriving via ⇢ tuner from the lab, the exact mix under test is shown above the table and Apply is disabled (it's not your applied targets). The stamp says HYPOTHETICAL.
  • Idealize tether 1:1 — assumption toggle: treat a USD-stable tether as a perfect $1 peg (erases real wobble/depegs, e.g. USDC's 2023 SVB dip). Stated in the stamp when on; refuses non-USD-stable tethers.

5. Composition lab (mix search)

Scopes

  • Full universe — your holdings + top market-cap candidates (venue-tradable, full-window history), searching groups AND weights.
  • Current holdings — re-weight only — your exact asset set, nothing added or dropped; each asset 4–80% on a 1% grid; every split carries its own split × sensitivity strip (per-X edge, best X starred — trust runs of green, not a lone starred spike).
  • Current holdings — allow drops — subsets + weights of what you own, so dead weight can be shed.

How to read a result

  • Two modes. With deep history the search runs regime mode (columns above: per-market-type harvest, classified from BTC's action into bull/bear/range swaths). With shallow history it runs folds mode (walk-forward: Folds ✓ / Median edge columns).
  • Harvest edge ≠ return. Bull/Bear/Range/Worst/Avg measure what rebalancing added over holding the same bag — blind to whether the bag itself sank. That's why the quality gate exists:
  • ✓mkt / ▼mkt — does the mix keep up with the market (holding BTC in your index currency) on return AND drawdown? ★ requires harvesting and ✓mkt with a positive holdout. Mixes that harvested well but sank are hidden (counted in the stamp).
  • No-harvest / no-real-possibility verdicts — when nothing clears the bar the table says so honestly instead of dressing up the least-bad row.
  • The stamp (bottom line) records everything: combos searched, universe and every exclusion by name (venue, missing history, your pool unchecks), window and what limited it, regime day-counts, the market bar, and any active assumptions.

6. Candidate pool (exclusions)

Candidate pool… (next to Run search) lists everything the lab would consider — held assets (marked •) and market candidates. Uncheck an asset to exclude it from every scope; saved per profile instantly, and named in the result stamp ("excluded by you: …"). The tethered index is locked — every mix is denominated in it. Use this to weed assets that are expensive to trade from your position (indirect crosses) or that you never want proposed.

7. Exchange sync

Link a read-only API key (Kraken or Bitso). Balances and trades sync automatically; your quantities become ground truth from the venue. Detected deposits/withdrawals wait for confirmation before splicing the track record (auto-apply once you trust it). Real fills also calibrate the fee/spread costs the backtests use. The sync note warns when the key lacks history permissions.

8. Safety rails

  • Buy-freeze (🧊) — an asset in a structural break (deep drawdown beyond its recovered envelope, or falling far more than the market) has BUY alerts suppressed; sells still alert. Auto-unfreezes on recovery or catch-up; click the badge to unfreeze manually.
  • Ignore freeze — standing per-asset override (visible on every active asset): BUY alerts and lab eligibility stay active even if the rail freezes it. Persists across freeze/unfreeze cycles — the chip highlights when armed.
  • Depeg watch (⚠) — a stable asset trading outside its band is flagged; valuation stays pinned 1:1.

9. Notifications

Per-profile recipients: email and/or Telegram (one bot token shared app-wide; each recipient /starts the bot, then "Find chat IDs"). The state machine: armed → alert sent → notified (quiet) → re-armed by the next sync, a screenshot upload, "Poll now", or the 12-hour timeout. "Poll now" is the universal reset.

10. Glossary

Sensitivity X
The profile-wide price-move threshold (%). Weight-normalized per asset into its Trigger @.
Harvest edge
Value growth above holding the identical basket untouched — what rebalancing itself added, in percentage points.
Hold
The do-nothing baseline: same starting weights, no trades ever.
Holdout
The most recent ~20% of the window, never touched during search/optimization — the only true out-of-sample number.
In-sample
The first ~80%, where the search optimizes. Bull/Bear/Range columns are in-sample attribution.
Regime / swath
Contiguous bull, bear or range stretch of the market, classified from BTC's action (centered trend, no lag). "Types ✓ 3/3" = harvested in all three.
Worst / Avg
Worst market-type edge (consistency — the primary rank) and the equal-weighted mean across types.
Folds
Shallow-history alternative: N sequential walk-forward windows; Folds ✓ counts how many the mix harvested in.
✓mkt
Keeps up with the market benchmark — holding BTC in the index currency — on both return and max drawdown.
Max DD
Deepest peak-to-trough fall of portfolio value across the window.
Basket
Chained unit-growth series moved only by trading; splice-continuous across targets, flows, and index switches.
Idealize tether
Assumption toggle: a USD-stable tether treated as a perfect $1 peg. Always stated in the stamp when active.
