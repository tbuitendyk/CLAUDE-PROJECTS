    Composition lab (mix search)

    Searches thousands of asset groups and target-weight combinations (your holdings + the top market-cap candidates with full-window history) for mixes where rebalancing genuinely beats holding. Ranked by the out-of-sample window the search never optimized on. Advisory only — adopting a mix means adding the assets and using "Set new targets" yourself.

    Current holdings only keeps your exact asset set (nothing added or dropped) and searches just the split — every asset 4–80% on a 1% grid — with each split scored jointly across the full sensitivity range, so you get the best allocation and its sensitivity together.

    Uncheck an asset to exclude it from every composition-lab scope (e.g. assets that are expensive to trade from your position — indirect crosses). Saved per profile, applied before any search starts. The tethered index can't be excluded — every mix is denominated in it.
