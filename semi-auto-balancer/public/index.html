    Composition lab (mix search)

    Searches thousands of asset groups and target-weight combinations (your holdings + the top market-cap candidates with full-window history) for mixes where rebalancing genuinely beats holding. Mixes range from a single tradable asset (tether + one coin, the coin taking 75–90%) up to 8 assets — the per-asset cap scales with the count (25% at 4+, wider for concentrated mixes). Ranked by the out-of-sample window the search never optimized on. Advisory only — adopting a mix means adding the assets and using "Set new targets" yourself.

    Current holdings only keeps your exact asset set (nothing added or dropped) and searches just the split — every asset 4–80% on a 1% grid — with each split scored jointly across the full sensitivity range, so you get the best allocation and its sensitivity together.

    Uncheck an asset to exclude it from every composition-lab scope (e.g. assets that are expensive to trade from your position — indirect crosses). Click an asset's 📌 to pin it: every searched mix must contain all pinned assets, so the search answers "what works best with my core set" — a pin overrides an exclusion and even keeps a pinned coin that drifted below the market-cap cut in the universe. Saved per profile, applied before any search starts. The tethered index can't be excluded or pinned — every mix is denominated in it. Dimmed entries are currently outside the top-40 market-cap cut a search draws from — ranks drift between snapshots, so they may enter a future run; exclusions and pins stick by asset regardless.
