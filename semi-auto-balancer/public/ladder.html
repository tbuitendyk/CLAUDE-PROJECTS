A two-asset (USD + BTC) strategy laboratory for the cycle-ladder thesis: buy rungs on drawdowns from the all-time high, sell rungs into strength anchored to the high you fell from, with order sizing, a hard USD reserve floor, and optional DCA. Parameters are tuned over the entire history — deliberately no holdout — and the forward test is the synthetic-futures grid below. Costs 0.5%/leg throughout. Advisory only.
