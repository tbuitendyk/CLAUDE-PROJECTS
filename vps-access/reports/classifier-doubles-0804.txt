THE THING I HAVE TO WALK BACK FIRST

You asked for the control-delta column a few days ago, I built it, and I
reported off it. On the replication table it is sound. On the search
leaderboard it is not, and I should have caught that before putting numbers
in front of you.

The mechanism: every row on that board is the WINNER of a search over the
whole execution menu - every gate, every bracket distance, every exit hour,
every quorum rung. The control is the best "always" (model-free) cell out of
that same menu. So the control is one of the candidates the winner already
beat. If the winner is a gated cell it beat the control by definition; if the
winner is an always cell it IS the control and the column shows a dash.

That is exactly what the finished board shows: 32 of the top 50 rows won with
a gate and all 32 are positive, the other 18 won ungated and show a dash. Not
one negative anywhere. That is not a result, it is arithmetic.

Where the same subtraction IS real: the replication table. There the cell is
DECLARED before the run and cannot be swapped for a better one, while the
control still gets its full 35-cell search - so the comparison actually runs
against the candidate and can come out negative. That is why Run 1's "5 of 17
positive vs control" was a meaningful, and damning, number for the DOT
configuration. That reading stands.

I have relabelled the column on the board, rewritten its tooltip, added a
footnote under the table and a paragraph in the help. Nothing about how it is
computed changed - only what it claims.
