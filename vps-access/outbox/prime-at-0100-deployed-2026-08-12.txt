What changed
------------
You asked to prime execution for 01:00 rather than pull the trigger five
minutes late. I added two wall-clock timers on top of the existing schedule:

  * VPS at 01:00:15 UTC — produces the day's intent with the LIVE 01:00 open
    (fetched through the Mexico tunnel, the same source that trained F1) and
    ships it to the box.
  * Box at 01:01:00 UTC — the executor fills it, ~1 minute after the open.

Before, the first produce was the :05 tick and the first fill the :10 exec, so
the entry landed ~10 minutes into the hour. Now it lands ~1 minute in — much
closer to the trained open, which is the whole point of the fidelity pilot.

The old :05 / :00-10 timers stay exactly as they were, as the backstop: if the
01:00 timers ever miss (tunnel down, box asleep), the ordinary tick ships the
intent a few minutes later. A miss is only ever a slightly LATER fill, never a
wrong one — stale data can only withhold a trade, never fabricate one.

Safety, in plain terms
----------------------
  * Box STOPPED. The timers are just alarm clocks; the master switch (ARM)
    still gates every entry. Right now ARM is absent, so at 01:01 the executor
    wakes, reconciles, and opens nothing. Deploy output confirmed: "master
    switch (ARM) present? NO — engine STOPPED until owner presses START".
  * No double trade. The 01:01 fire and the 01:10 fire both run the executor,
    but it de-duplicates by the period key, so a period can only ever open
    once, in any order.
  * The two entry timers deliberately do NOT catch up after downtime — a
    retro-fire at, say, 03:00 would fill hours past the trained open, which is
    worthless. Only the boundary fire counts.

If you want to take today's 01:00 entry: press START before ~01:30 UTC (the
intent goes stale 30 min after it is produced). The plan on the status screen
is the day's preview. If you'd rather wait for a fresh day, just leave it
stopped and arm whenever you like — tomorrow's 01:00 will prime itself.
