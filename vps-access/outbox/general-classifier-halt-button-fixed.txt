The halt is cleared, and I found why your button was doing nothing

tl;dr Your setup is unhalted right now. The button was genuinely broken, not
being ignored — the machine only accepts a clear request for 15 minutes, and the
thing that delivered it ran once an hour, so almost every press you made arrived
too late and was thrown away without telling you. Fixed, tested, and running.

WHY IT DID NOTHING

Three faults stacked, and each one hid the next.

First, the timing was impossible. When you press the button, a request is
written and something has to carry it to the trading machine. The machine only
accepts a request that is less than 15 minutes old. The thing carrying it ran
once an hour. So unless you happened to press in the last few minutes before the
hour turned, your request arrived 20, 40, 55 minutes old and the machine
correctly refused it as too old.

This is written in the machine's own log. At 08:08 it recorded a refusal with
the age attached: 2567 seconds. That is 43 minutes, against a 15 minute limit.
It happened again at 16:08 with the request you made at 15:22 — 45 minutes.

The equivalent button for the whole machine has always worked, because its
request is carried every 5 minutes. Yours was put in the wrong place and
inherited an hourly schedule that cannot meet the 15-minute rule.

Second, a refusal was recorded as a success. Whatever the machine decided, the
carrying step reported "done" and deleted your request. So each press was thrown
away by its own failure.

Third, nothing told you. There was no message anywhere saying a clear had been
refused, so the only thing left to do was press it again — which is what you
did, and it could never have worked.

WHAT I CHANGED

Your button's request now rides the same 5-minute carry as the machine-wide one.
That is the actual fix; the rest is making the failure impossible to hide again.

The machine now reports a refusal as a refusal instead of as success, so a
rejected request is no longer deleted as though it had been delivered. Your
screen now shows two things it never showed: that a clear is written and waiting
to be collected, and — if the machine turns one down — that it was refused and
the reason it gave. Pressing again replaces an old refusal so you are never
looking at two conflicting messages.

The screen also used to say your request would be carried "on its next sync (a
few minutes)". That sentence was written for the machine-wide button and copied
onto yours, where it described something that could not happen. Both now say the
same thing, and it is now true of both.

One more, found while fixing this: the manual fallback I use when a button
fails could only clear a machine-wide halt. It could not reach a setup's halt at
all — so when your button was failing, the backup could not reach it either.
Same lock-out, different door. It takes a setup name now.

THE HALT ITSELF

Cleared by hand, at 16:12 UTC. The record marks it as a hand clear by me rather
than a press by you, because that is what it was and the log should not blur
the two.

Your two trades are untouched and still close on schedule. The master switch was
not touched. If the reason for the halt were still live, the next check would
halt it again — that is the check working, and it has not.

HOW I CHECKED IT

I wrote a read-only trace that walks the whole chain and prints what every link
actually holds; that is what turned "the button does nothing" into the 2567
seconds above, rather than me guessing. It now also asserts the carry has not
drifted back to the hourly schedule, and says so loudly if it ever does.

Seven new tests. I broke the fix on purpose and watched each one fail before
trusting it.
