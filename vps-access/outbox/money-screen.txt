Money screen: 3 setups clear the cost floor, and the leader survives fees nearly 4x what we pay
tl;dr: I ran the declared money screen. Three of the fifty scored setups clear "positive after fees AND 50% cost headroom", and the leader is in a different league from the arm we spent all day confirming: $158.32 net on 224 trades, break-even fee $0.4784 per leg against the $0.125 we pay - it would still make money at nearly four times our costs. It also wins 56.7% of its trades against 49.0% for the confirmed arm. Two things stop this being good news yet: it searched only 0.2% of the board, and the window it looks good on is the same window I selected it on, so that window is spent as evidence. The only honest test is data no run has touched, and we now have about five weeks of it.

WHY THE LEADER IS DIFFERENT, and it is instructive: it trades only 224 of
364 periods (62%) where the confirmed arm trades 85%. It stands aside far
more often, and that is exactly where the cost headroom comes from. Fewer,
better trades beat more, thinner ones once fees are real.

THE HONEST CAVEATS, which matter more than the numbers:
- COVERAGE 0.2%. Only 50 of 20,400 units ever get a held-back score,
  because scoring it is reserved for setups already promoted on the
  search window. That is the right design - it keeps the held-back
  window clean - but it means this screen looked at fifty setups, not
  twenty thousand.
- THE WINDOW IS SPENT. I selected this row BY its held-back money, so
  that money can no longer serve as evidence for it. No null test on
  that window repairs this.
- I HAD SEEN IT BEFORE. The run A launcher names this exact row and
  declines to chase it as menu re-shopping. The screen was declared in
  advance; the row was not unseen. Calling it a fresh discovery would
  be false.
- It is a 4-member committee, so it cannot be reproduced by the
  8-member path. Substituting 1-of-8 would be testing a different setup
  and calling it the same one.

WHAT SETTLES IT: the cache now runs to 2026-08-02/06 while every window
above ends 2026-06 - about five weeks nothing has touched. That is only
~9 periods and maybe 5-6 trades, far too thin for a verdict, and I will
report it as thin. Its value is being the first honest forward record,
and a forward record is what a Binance test-trade decision should rest
on rather than any backtest.

MY NEXT MOVES: build a forward-scoring path that takes a frozen setup
(including 4-member ones) and scores it over a named date window, then
pre-register all three clearers as frozen forward books BEFORE any
forward number exists, so the record cannot be re-picked afterwards.
