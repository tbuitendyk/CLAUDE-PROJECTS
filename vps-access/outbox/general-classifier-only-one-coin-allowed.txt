So the gate is enforcing a rule against a limit that no longer exists.

WHY I DID NOT JUST OPEN IT UP

Because the real limit moved rather than disappeared. Trading a second coin
needs that coin enabled and funded for borrowing on the sub-account at Binance.
That is a fact about your account, not about this code. If I simply widened the
list, you would be able to start a setup that the exchange then refused — I
would have traded a clear "no" now for a confusing failure later, which is
worse.

YOUR CALL, TWO WAYS

1. Make the list yours. It becomes a field you edit per trading machine: you
   say which coins that machine serves. Small change, in your hands today, and
   it matches how everything else now works.

2. Ask the exchange instead. The machine reports which coins are actually
   enabled for borrowing on its sub-accounts, and the list is built from that.
   No typed-in list anywhere, and it cannot go stale the way this one did.

Option 2 is the better build and removes the last typed-in coin list from the
live path. Option 1 is quicker and unblocks you now. They are not exclusive —
1 then 2 is reasonable.
