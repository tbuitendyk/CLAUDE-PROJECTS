THE RANKED LIST

One declared config: the table stands alone, exactly as it was. Nothing to choose
between, so a list would be furniture.

Many: a ranked list comes first, scrollable, one line per configuration, each
opening to that configuration on every asset.

Table: what each line of the ranked list shows.
Key — held up on: assets whose once-only held-back look made money; 1-in-N: how
unlikely that split is on coin flips; beat always-long: same count against forcing
the position long every period; total held-back: summed money on the once-only
look.

  column            what it tells you
  held up on k/n    the count that IS the replication claim
  1-in-N            how unlikely that count is by chance alone
  beat always-long  did the calls earn their keep, or was it just being long
  total held-back   the money, last on purpose

The ranking leads on how unlikely the across-asset split is, then the share of
assets that held up, and money is the LAST tiebreak. That order matters: sorting
by money would rebuild exactly the shopped board the whole replication idea
exists to avoid. A configuration that held on 9 of 10 assets outranks one that
made more dollars on 2 of 10.
