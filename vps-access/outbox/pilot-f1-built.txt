WHAT IS BUILT AND TESTED (no keys, no live orders, all committed)

- The executor (vps-access/executor/mx_executor.py): deterministic, pure
  standard library, keeps an append-only journal that IS its memory, reconciles
  the exchange against that journal every run, and fails safe — on anything odd
  it halts new entries but still runs due exits. Kill rules are all in, each
  labelled a guess so we sharpen them with real data: 3 rejects in a row, a fill
  more than 1% off the decision price, an exchange/journal mismatch, a missed
  exit, or cumulative loss past $50. 15 tests drive the whole path against a
  fake Binance on localhost, including the live-order signing path.

- The signal producer (general-classifier/lib/pilotsignal.js): computes the
  current committee call by reusing the EXACT frozen F1 members the forward book
  uses, so every live fill has a paper twin. It emits an intent carrying only a
  direction and the decision price — never a size, so a bad intent can point a
  fixed $10 clip the wrong way but can never enlarge it.

  plus the two VPS timer scripts that ship intents to the box and pull the
