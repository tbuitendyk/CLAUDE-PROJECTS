Entry retry protocol live: a failed entry now gets 6 tries over the hour

tl;dr — two changes are deployed. (1) The decision history was dating rows by
the feature-window start, 4 days before the trade they drove, which made a
complete record look abandoned; rows now carry the entry they acted on. (2) Per
your directive, a failed entry retries up to 6 times until one hour past its
moment, then gives up out loud instead of silently forfeiting the day. Both

THE RETRY CHANGE

Before: an entry got ONE real attempt. The intent was consumed before the price
was fetched and the order sent, so anything failing after that point — no price
that run, a rejected order — forfeited the trade the model called, with nothing
on screen saying a trade had been missed. Failures before that point got three
10-minute ticks, because the intent died at 30 minutes.

Now: the intent is kept until the period is finished. Attempts are counted in
the journal and capped at 6; the window is one hour, anchored on the entry
moment itself (the producer now stamps it, so an intent minted late gets no
extra life); one attempt per period per tick. If the hour runs out or the six
are spent, the box writes ENTRY_GAVE_UP — an incident on the screen and a page
from the alerter. A missed entry is now as loud as a bad one.

The money invariant is unchanged and I hardened the test around it: at most one
fill per period, ever. A period that filled, that has an order possibly live at
the venue, or that was drift-killed is terminal and never re-attempted. Every
attempt also reuses the same deterministic order id, so a resend of something
the venue already filled is refused as a duplicate rather than filling twice.

TWO THINGS YOU SHOULD KNOW, NOT BURIED

1. A REJECT-driven retry tops out at 3, not 6. Three consecutive rejects halt
   the box — that safety kill predates this and I did not touch it. So the six
   attempts are fully available for transport and price failures, but a venue
   refusing the order will trip the reject-kill first. That threshold is
   labelled GUESSED in the register. Say the word and I will look at whether it
   should be derived.

2. I made a judgement call on what counts as retryable. Transient failures
   retry: no price this run, a rejected order, a clip momentarily under the
   exchange minimum. Deliberate refusals do not: the price-drift kill, a
   concurrency cap, an allowlist failure. Overrule any of those and I will
   change it.
