WHAT HALTED, AND WHY IT WAS NOT A FAULT IN YOUR ACCOUNT

At 03:20 the box stopped itself with:

  reconcile mismatch: borrow 0.221886 below journal shorts 0.224000

The reconcile check compares what the exchange says you BORROWED against what
the journal says you SOLD SHORT. Those match only when the borrow financed the
whole sale. Your account was holding 0.00211675 LTC of leftover dust — a
residue too small to sell, because Binance refuses orders under $5. When the
entry sold 0.224, it spent that dust first and borrowed only the rest:

  0.224 − 0.00211675 = 0.22188325   ...which is the 0.221886 it complained about.

So the money was correct to the eighth decimal. What was wrong was the check.
The LONG side of that same comparison has tolerated exactly this dust since an
earlier review; the SHORT side never got the same tolerance and was still
comparing against a flat 0.001. The halt was arithmetically unavoidable and it
fired in the safe direction — it stopped NEW entries and left your open short
and its scheduled exit completely alone.

Fixed: the short side now gets the same dust-sized tolerance as the long side,
derived from the same quantity rather than a second invented constant. Verified
on the live box, not just in tests: 03:40 said RECONCILE_MISMATCH and 03:50 said
RECONCILE_OK on identical numbers.

CLEARING A HALT FROM THE SCREEN

You asked for a way to get out of a halt without needing me. There is now a
"Clear the halt" button in the halt banner on the Trading tab. It states the
recorded reason before it does anything, and it does NOT start trading — the
master switch still governs entries, and if the cause is unfixed the next check
halts again, which is the check working.

I tested it the only way that means anything: set a real halt on the live box
and cleared it through the actual button. It cleared in about two seconds. I
also confirmed that pressing it twice cannot clear a SECOND, later halt — a
spent request is refused.

Two things I got wrong in the first version, found by checking my own work:

  1. The button could not have worked at all. The helper script that carries
     the request to the box was never added to the installer, so the request
     was written and nothing ever picked it up. Worse, it failed SILENTLY: you
     would have pressed it, seen nothing happen, and had nothing to go on. It
     now installs, and the carry prints a loud diagnostic if that file is ever
     missing again.
  2. The request carried a signature that nothing checked — a path that LOOKS
     authenticated and is not. Clearing a halt removes a safety brake, so it
     now gets the same treatment as the START switch: signed, time-limited,
     and impossible to replay. The STOP switch stays deliberately unsigned,
     because an unsigned STOP must always still stop.

FEE/LEG: MODEL — you were right

The modelled fee was the $100-clip figure while the pilot trades $10 clips. A
per-leg fee in dollars means nothing without the clip it is charged on, so the
screen was over-stating modelled cost by 10x next to live numbers. It is now
derived as a RATE from the one shared constant and re-applied to the live clip
size, so the two cannot drift apart. The clip it assumes is named on screen.
