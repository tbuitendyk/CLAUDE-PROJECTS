THE HEADLINE DEFECT (and why the calibration failed)
The walk-forward design weighted recent history heavier by COPYING the
last two years of training rows. The copies also landed in the slice each
model uses to check itself while training — so the models were grading
themselves on questions they had already memorized, and their tuning knobs
were set too loose. That is exactly what the fake-coin test caught: the
coin whose signal dies mid-history scored the same in its alive era as on
pure noise, because the mistuned models could not tell the difference.
Weighting is now removed entirely; it only comes back as proper per-row
weights, never by copying rows. (QC 58.)

THE OTHER SEVEN, in one line each:
- The job list silently hid walk-forward runs (crashed on their format).
- A restart could mislabel dead jobs as alive for the same reason. (QC 59)
- The weekly-8d shape can never score a walk-forward fold; it used to
  fail every fold and blame "data gap". Now it is refused up front with
  the real reason, and skip messages only name causes that can actually
  happen. (QC 61)
- The web endpoint dropped a launcher's custom execution menu — a
  narrowed run silently ran the full default menu instead.
- A mis-typed fee or trade floor was silently "corrected" to a default;
  now it refuses loudly, naming the field and the bad value. (QC 60)
- The safety margin that keeps a fold's trades out of the next slice was
  3 hours short in one configuration.
- Assorted smaller ones: cancelled runs now keep their finished units,
  the median is computed without a small upward/downward bias, an open
  fold table no longer vanishes every 5 seconds, failed setups are shown
  as MISSING on the board instead of silently absent.

Full list with tests: commit 6846bd0; QC register items 58-62.
