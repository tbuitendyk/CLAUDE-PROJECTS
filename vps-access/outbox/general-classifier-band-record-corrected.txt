WHAT WAS WRONG

The dormant band — the flat zone around zero inside which a member votes "no
move" — was typed into a source file by hand as 1.69. The discovery run's actual
value was 1.6858050329831362. You replaced the transcription with the real
number.

That band is one of the ingredients baked into each decision's input_hash, a
fingerprint the mirror uses to prove a recorded decision still reproduces. So
every decision recorded before the correction carried a fingerprint computed
from a number that was never the rule, and the mirror refused to reproduce them.
It was right to refuse.

PROOF, NOT INFERENCE

I recomputed each fingerprint from that decision's own recorded votes, side,
quorum, symbol and training cutoff, once with each band:

  date        fingerprint on file   = sha(band 1.69)?   = sha(band 1.6858..)?
  2026-08-08  4d31248286863d6d      YES                 no
  2026-08-14  af64a9f7d8eba810      no                  YES
  2026-08-15  69e1f0653f70cc81      no                  YES

Every fingerprint reproduces exactly from one band or the other, so the band is
the entire difference and nothing else drifted. KEY: "fingerprint" is the
input_hash field, a 16-character digest; "sha(band X)" means the digest that the
same decision would produce under band X. This is not money — no P&L number
changes here, and no trade changes.

I owe you a correction. Last time I told you my band diagnosis was probably
wrong, because 08-08 had matched on an earlier check and then broke. That
retraction was itself wrong. The band was the cause all along; what actually
happened is that my first repair pass rewrote 08-14 and 08-15 and skipped 08-08,
so 08-08 surfaced afterwards looking like a new problem.
