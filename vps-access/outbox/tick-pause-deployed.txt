Why nothing is lost by a pause: the books are deterministic on candle
history — a skipped tick just means the next tick after the batch ends
reads the same candles and settles the same trades with the same
numbers, only later on the clock. The books' own self-heal path (missed
days arrive flagged, never lost) is the mechanism that guarantees it.
