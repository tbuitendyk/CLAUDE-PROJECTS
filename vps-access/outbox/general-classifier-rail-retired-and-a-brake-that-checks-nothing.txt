THE THING I WANT YOU TO DECIDE ON

Your profile's reproduce-check — the thing that recomputes a past decision and
checks the engine still makes the same call — reports this right now:

    ok: true | breaks: 0 | checked: 0

"checked: 0" is the whole story. Every decision record on the profile came
across from the old engine and is stamped with that engine's version. The check
counts those separately rather than calling them all breaks (that was the right
fix — otherwise all 8 read as failures and the profile got halted). But the
profile has not yet written a record under its own version, so after that
filtering there is nothing left to examine. It checks zero, finds zero problems,
and reports clean.

There is already a guard for exactly this: an alert that fires when the check
verified nothing WHILE a position is open. It is on the box-level alerter — the
one reading the retired file that nothing writes. The alerter watching the live
file pages on breaks, on errors, and if the file goes stale, but not on a check
that examined nothing.

So: two open shorts, no drift protection, and silence. The trades themselves are
fine and their exits are scheduled as normal — this is the instrument, not the
positions. It resolves on its own the first time the profile produces a decision
under its own version, which is the next entry. I would rather you knew than
have it quietly fix itself and never be told it happened.

Three ways to go, your pick:
  1. Add the checked:0 guard to the live alerter. Small, and it makes the gap
     audible from now on rather than something I happened to notice.
  2. Leave it — it clears itself at the next entry.
  3. Something else you have in mind.
