THE NEW TOOL, and what it prints:
  - Headline: flat $ vs ladder $ over every priced historical entry, and the
    uplift.
  - TABLE: $ by agreement bucket. KEY — agreement: members voting the winning
    side that day; mult: the declared clip multiplier; trades: entries in the
    bucket (⚠ = fewer than 10, too thin to lean on); wins: trades with
    positive net $; flat $ / ladder $: bucket profit-and-loss in dollars at
    1x vs at the multiplied clip.
  - Chance check: 1000 shuffles of WHICH trades got which agreement (returns
    fixed, bucket sizes preserved, seeded). If random deals match the real
    ladder too often, the verdict says so.
  - Exposure honesty: return per deployed dollar (a 4x ladder deploys more
    capital, so raw $ flatters it), worst single trade at ladder size, deepest
    drawdown, and PEAK CONCURRENT notional — the wallet must fund that number
    before any of it is real. For reference: 4x on $10 clips at 6 concurrent
    positions is $240 against ~$190 in the sub-account today.
  - Verdict is three-valued (YES on this history / not distinguishable from
    chance / inconclusive on thin buckets); the thresholds are labelled
    GUESSED — prompts to look harder, never a retirement of the idea on one
    number.
