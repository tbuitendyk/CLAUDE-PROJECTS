setup, it measures the setup plus however much history you happened to
feed it. For F2 the second part dominated completely. That is registered
as QC 83 and is now a standing gate - nothing reaches a live-money
conversation without passing it, because a number from an unstable setup
is one draw from a range nobody looked at.
