THE HYPOTHESIS BEHIND CYCLE 1

Every hunt we have run ranked cells by MONEY. That fuses two questions:

    does the committee predict?        does the execution monetise it?

A null on money could always have been either, which is why four nights of
work kept producing ambiguous corpses. The acc/edge columns you now have make
the first question askable on its own — and it is the one that matters,
because if there is no prediction anywhere then no amount of execution tuning
can help, and we should stop tuning execution and go looking somewhere else
entirely.

So cycle 1 is not a P&L screen. 17 assets x 5 chunk shapes x both decision
modes, holdout on, trailing off. The only number that counts is holdout edge
at the rung chosen by EDGE on the search window. Select on search, judge on
holdout.

WHY I AM DISCARDING THE FIRST RESULT

It came back 20 of 29 positive on holdout edge, p = 0.03. That is exactly the
shape of a finding and it is not one.

The board is ranked by money and capped at fifty rows. So those 29 were the
P&L winners of 170 units — and a unit wins on P&L partly BECAUSE its calls
were good. Measuring edge on the money winners selects on edge. The number is
biased upward by construction.

What annoys me about it is that I built the edge-selected rung an hour earlier
specifically to stop money from choosing what I then evaluate as a predictor,
and then read the result through a money-ranked board anyway. Same error, one
level up.

The screen now promotes every unit and writes a census row per unit that never
touches the leaderboard. Re-running as bracketlab-20260728-2211.

READING RULE, FIXED BEFORE THE RESULT EXISTS

 - Roughly half of 170 units should be positive on holdout edge by chance.
 - A chunk shape or decision mode is interesting only if positive on a clear
   majority of its 17 assets — and even then it is a LEAD owing its own
   declared test, not a result.
 - The pooled line mixes chunk shapes, decision modes and correlated assets. I

 - If holdout edge is a coin flip everywhere: that closes execution tuning as
