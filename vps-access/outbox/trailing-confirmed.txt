Trailing confirmed at minute resolution — the assumption was worth 13%

tl;dr — All four items from your list are now done and exercised on real data. The headline number: on the first trailing cell, the hourly run had to guess the intra-bar order on 23 bars, and settling those with minute data moved the result by $15.05 — about 13% of the cell's P&L. The ambiguity count fell from 23 to 1. New standing rule: no trailing result gets quoted without minute confirmation.

THE MEASUREMENT

DOTUSDT, daily-3d, declared always-gate, distance 1x band, 65-hour exit,
trailing 1x band with no arm delay, quorum 4 of 12. Minute coverage 99.8%.

                net P&L   trades   wins   stops   trail-amb
    hourly      -118.92      427    156     424          23
    minute      -103.87      427    154     424           1
    delta        +15.05       +0             +0

Read it like this:

 - trail-amb 23 -> 1 is the mechanism working. Twenty-two of the twenty-three
   "which came first" questions are settled outright by minute data. The one
   survivor is a single minute in which the same thing happens again — which
   is the honest limit: this shrinks the ambiguity roughly sixtyfold, it does
   not abolish it.

 - $15.05 on a cell making -$118.92 is about 13%. That is not a rounding
   error. It is the size of what an hourly trailing number is resting on, and
   it is the reason this step is not optional.

 - The delta came out FAVOURABLE here, meaning the pessimistic assumption was
   too harsh in this instance. That is not good news and I would not report it
   as such. The hourly bar hides the ordering in both directions, so
   confirmation can just as easily take money away. A favourable delta is
   evidence of nothing except that the assumption was binding.

 - The cell loses either way, -118.92 to -103.87. Confirmation does not rescue
   a bad setup, it just tells you what the setup actually was. And it stops out
   on 424 of 427 trades because an arm of 0 with a 1x trail puts the stop one
   band below entry immediately. I picked that config to generate stop
   activity for the test, not on merit. Nothing about it is a finding.

NEW STANDING RULE, NOW IN VERDICTS.MD

No trailing result is quotable without minute confirmation. The hourly figure
is an estimate resting on an assumption, and trail-amb is the measure of how
much of it rests there. A static-stop result barely depends on this; a
trailing one depends on it constantly.
