HOW THE VERIFICATION ACTUALLY WORKS, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Both mailboxes are on the same server, so a real message from you is an
authenticated submission and Postfix writes that down. I take the Message-ID
off the mail, find its queue ID in the mail log, and require a login by your
mailbox for it. Mail injected from outside arrives with no login recorded and
cannot fake one without your password. What that proves is that your MAILBOX
sent it — not that you typed it. If those credentials were ever stolen, this
check would pass, so it is worth keeping the password fresh.
