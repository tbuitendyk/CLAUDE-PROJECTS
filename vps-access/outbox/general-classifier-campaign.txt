The campaign list: nothing was missing, the control was wrong. Fixed and deployed.

tl;dr — you have THREE campaigns on the box and the service was offering all
three. The control only ever showed you one, and it was never going to show you
more. Fixed, along with the two coverage gaps I flagged as open in my last mail.
Deployed 02:14 UTC, 498 tests pass, 47 tab views clean on three consecutive runs.

WHY IT SHOWED ONE

The campaign box was a text field with a suggestion list attached, and the field
is pre-filled with your current campaign. A suggestion list FILTERS itself by
what is already in the field. So it offered the one name that matched what was
already typed, and the other two were unreachable unless you cleared the box
first.

Nothing was broken and nothing was missing. The control was simply the wrong
kind of control for the job.

What is on the box, for the record:

  campaign                              runs carrying it
  test1...solo asset broad sweep        22
  test3...triple asset broad sweep      13   (currently set)
  test2...dual asset broad sweep         1
  (no campaign)                         93

Table: campaigns on this box. KEY — runs carrying it: how many saved runs were
launched while that campaign was set. The 93 with none are runs fired before a
campaign was in force; they are not lost, they are simply unfiled.

WHAT IT IS NOW

Two plain controls side by side: a dropdown listing every campaign the box has
ever stamped, newest activity first, and the text box for naming a NEW one.
Picking from the dropdown switches to it immediately. The line beneath says which
is set and how many exist.

This is the same fault as the run-id boxes I fixed yesterday — where the service
can list the choices, the screen should show them, not make you type or guess.

THE TWO GAPS I SAID WERE OPEN

Both closed, and closing them found two more real faults.

The Trading channel controls now have runtime coverage: Activate, Deactivate and
Nuke are actually pressed in a browser. The pass checks that CANCELLING the
Deactivate confirmation changes nothing — a confirmation that does not protect is
worse than none — that confirming changes the state and the row says so, and that
Nuke refuses a config whose channel is still active.

The Trading money screen is now checked against its own endpoint: realized,
unrealized, the open-position count and the table must all match the side being
shown, and showing the other book's money is called out by name.

WHAT THAT FOUND

1. The setup screen's Incidents panel was blind to most of what the executor
   reports. It showed four kinds; the executor journals a dozen with a setup id
   on them. So a rejected order, an order whose outcome is UNKNOWN, an overdue
   exit, a stale intent, and a period the executor GAVE UP on all showed nothing
   — the panel read "none — clean" through a real failure. The F1 screen has
   shown all of these all along; only its generalized twin was missing them.

2. Re-activating a channel with positions still open is correctly refused, but
   the refusal counted both books into one number — it said "2 open" while the
   screen in front of you showed 1. Two numbers that cannot be reconciled. It
   names the split now.

AND ONE I COMMITTED MYSELF, WORTH SAYING

The first version of the controls pass deactivated the same channel the money
pass needed. It passed once and then silently SKIPPED both checks on every run
after. A check that quietly stops checking is exactly what this harness exists to
prevent, and I built that failure into the harness itself. It now mints its own
throwaway config, puts back anything it changes, and I verified repeatability by
running it three times and requiring identical output rather than reasoning that
it should be. Register entry 152.
