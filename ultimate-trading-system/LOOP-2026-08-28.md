## Success rules, committed in advance

A step is DONE only when its rule below is met. No rule is rewritten after
seeing a number.

- **S1 — no dead numbers.** At every one of the five chunk shapes, and for a
  coin on its own / alongside one / alongside two: no measurement is frozen
  across chunks, and no measurement is an exact copy of another. Proved by a
  test that builds real chunks and checks both directions. TODAY this fails:
  2 frozen and 3 duplicated at Daily 1-day for a coin on its own.
- **S2 — the committee is what it claims.** For every unit, the number of
  INDEPENDENT voices is measured and recorded beside the member count. A
  member family that adds members without adding voices is visible as such.
  TODAY 16 of the owner's 49 units are 6 members and 3 voices, invisibly.
- **S3 — agreement rules are selectable and all reach the pricing.** Every
  agreement rule built is choosable in the interface, is recorded on the set,
  and produces a differently-named setting. No rule is reachable only from
  code (RULE FIVE).
- **S4 — old sets are refused, never mixed.** A record set built on the old
  measurements cannot become the parent of a new one. Refused BY NAME with a
  sentence saying why. Nothing of the owner's is deleted by me.
- **S5 — the suite is green and the guards bite.** Every new rule has a test,
  and every test has a mutation guard proving it fails when the guard is
  removed.
- **S6 — the twelve interface demands hold on both screens**, checked by the
  screen tests, not by eye.
