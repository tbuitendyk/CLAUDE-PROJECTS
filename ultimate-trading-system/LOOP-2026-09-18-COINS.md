## Pre-registered — written before the numbers exist

### A — what `best on both halves` should come back as

W-3 carries 15,912 rows over 18 coins, 13 bands and 21 look-backs. A pair is
one coin and one chunk shape. For a shape a fixed look-back walks, a pair holds
13 x 22 = **286 rows**; for a shape only `own` walks, 13.

If the two halves were unrelated, the row topping the early half would top the
late half about **1 time in 286** on a big pair. Over roughly 74 pairs that is
well under one.

**So, declared now:**

- **more than 3 pairs answering yes** is beyond chance and the column is worth
  selecting on.
- **0 or 1** means the column is honest and has nothing in it — and that is an
  answer about the axis, not a fault in the column. It gets reported as such and
  the tick stays, because a tick that keeps nothing is telling the truth.
- **2 or 3** is inconclusive and gets called inconclusive.

No other reading of that number is allowed after the fact.

### D1 — what a migrated set must satisfy before it is swapped in

- **Same row count**, exactly.
- **Same block boundaries**, so every stored block index still points where it
  did.
- **`confirm` gone from every row**, and no other field changed — spot-checked
  on the first, last and a middle row of each set.
- **Everything downstream deleted, not migrated** (RULE NINE): totals and any
  derived file are rebuilt from the migrated records.
- A set that fails any of these is **left alone** and named here.
