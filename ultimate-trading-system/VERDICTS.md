# Pre-registered verdicts

The results record for the Ultimate Trading System.

Nothing is recorded here yet. This system starts from zero use: the previous
record — three paper books, the execution pilot, and every sweep read between
2026-07-26 and 2026-08-19 — is closed and kept in `ARCHIVE/VERDICTS.md`.

Each entry is written in two parts. The success and failure rules go in first,
before the outcome can be known, and the git history is the timestamp that
proves it. The result goes in afterwards, read once against those rules and
recorded whichever way it fell.
