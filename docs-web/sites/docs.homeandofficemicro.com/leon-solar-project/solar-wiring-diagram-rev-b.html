Commissioning sequence

Note No pre-charge resistor needed. The LuxPower SNA-US-12K has integrated soft-start — its DC input circuit handles inrush internally via a controlled relay sequence. This is a meaningful upgrade from the older Chinese-direct PowMr designs.
  1. Confirm DC disconnect breaker is OFF. All AC breakers OFF. PV strings disconnected at inverter.
  2. Multimeter check: battery stack output at ~51–53 V resting (LCG controller breaker closed, BMS active, LCD on).
  3. Verify polarity at inverter battery terminals before energizing. Red probe should read battery voltage when black is momentarily on BAT−.
  4. Close the main DC disconnect. The LuxPower will run its internal soft-start: relays close in sequence, capacitors charge over ~2 seconds, LCD comes up.
  5. Power on the inverter. Configure: battery type = LITHIUM, brand profile = FELICITY (or LUX/EOS depending on firmware revision). Set discharge limit to 80% SOC, recharge target 100%.
  6. Plug Cat6 between LCG controller BMS port and inverter BMS port. Within 30 seconds the inverter LCD should display real-time SOC%. If it doesn't, check protocol setting and Cat6 pinout — Felicity uses standard CAN H/L on pins 4 and 5.
  7. Multimeter check each PV string at the inverter terminals (still disconnected): expect ~360–410 V open-circuit per string at sunlit STC.
  8. Connect strings one at a time to MPPT-1A, MPPT-1B, MPPT-2A, MPPT-2B. Each connection — verify polarity, twist MC4 to lock, confirm string voltage on LCD.
  9. Close AC grid input breaker (CFE side). LCD shows "Grid OK." Inverter performs anti-islanding check (~30 seconds), then bypass activates.
  10. Close AC load output breaker. Test with one small load first (lamp on one leg), then verify L1–L2 reads 240 V, L1–N and L2–N each read 120 V.
  11. Open LuxPower ShineMonitor or LuxView app on phone — register inverter via WiFi dongle. Confirm PV power, battery SOC, AC output all reporting.
  12. Run a 1-hour load test at 4–6 kW (turn on one or both z15s briefly). Watch battery current draw, inverter temperature, and AC output stability. Log baseline values.

Install gotchas

Gotcha 01 · Fuse rating Class T fuse sized at 300 A — LuxPower max battery current is 250 A, so 1.25× (NEC continuous duty multiplier) = 312.5 A rounded down to nearest standard size = 300 A. The Felicity stack itself can deliver 360 A continuous, so the fuse protects the inverter side, not the battery. Mounts on positive battery cable within 18 inches of the LCG controller output.
Gotcha 02 · Neutral–ground bonding (LuxPower-specific) LuxPower SNA-US-12K switches its N–G bond automatically. When off-grid (no CFE), it bonds N to G internally. When CFE is present and the bypass relay is closed, it disconnects its internal bond so CFE's bond at the service entrance is the only one. Do not bond N to G at your sub-panel — that creates a parallel bond and ground-loop currents. The load panel feeds floating L1/L2/N with separate G; bonding happens upstream only.
Gotcha 03 · PV string cold-day max VOC 8 panels × 47.5 V VOC = 380 V at STC. On a cold León morning (5 °C), VOC rises ~6% to ~404 V — comfortably under LuxPower's 480 V max. The MPPT operating window (panels at 50 °C) lands around 320 V, dead center of the 120–385 V MPPT range. Never extend strings beyond 8 panels; 9 in series gets you to ~454 V cold which is closer to the ceiling than I'd want.
Gotcha 04 · Bifacial gain requires maintenance The white reflective surface is what gets you 8–12% extra production from the back side of the panels. Sweep / hose the surface monthly. Dust and bird droppings drop albedo from ~80% to ~30% within weeks in León's dry season. Plan a quarterly recoat schedule if you used elastomeric paint — UV degrades it over time. Also: keep weeds/grass shorter than the elevation post height under the array.
Gotcha 05 · Inverter location & temperature LuxPower SNA-US-12K is rated 0–45 °C ambient, IP20 (indoor only). León summer attic temperatures hit 50–55 °C — the inverter will derate output above 45 °C and shut down above 60 °C. Mount in a conditioned indoor space (utility room, garage with cross-ventilation, basement) with at least 30 cm clearance on all sides. Don't enclose it in a tight cabinet without forced ventilation.
Gotcha 06 · Battery stack physical setup The 8-module Felicity tower is ~210 cm tall, ~400 kg, IP21 indoor only. Floor must support point loading (~50 kg per module). Required clearances: 60 cm in front (for service access to LCG breaker and LCD), 30 cm on each side, 30 cm above. Don't put it on carpet, against fabric walls, or in unconditioned spaces — operating temp range is 0–55 °C charge / −20 to +55 °C discharge.
Gotcha 07 · Lug torque schedule Same discipline as the previous design. Torque every termination to spec, re-check on schedule:
  • Inverter battery terminals (M8): 10–12 N·m
  • Class T fuse studs (M10): 14–16 N·m
  • DC breaker lugs: 5 N·m
  • Felicity stack interconnect (factory cables, hex bolt): per LCG manual, typically 8 N·m
  • AC terminals (L1, L2, N, G): per LuxPower manual, typically 3–4 N·m
  • Recheck at 30 days · 6 months · 12 months · then annually
Gotcha 08 · Future second stack expansion When you add the second battery stack (1× LCG + 3× LMG planned), you'll need to: (a) rebalance your current 8-module stack — physically move 4 modules from the existing tower to the new tower so both end up as 4-module stacks, (b) update inverter firmware if needed for multi-stack parallel comms, (c) re-cable the BMS CAN bus to daisy-chain both LCG controllers. Plan a half-day downtime for the migration. Coordinate with JM Energía.
