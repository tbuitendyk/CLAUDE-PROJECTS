3. Assembly sequence (codes + drawings)

Condensed from Rev. C §14 — that section carries the full QC gates. Torques and test loads get written into the QC sheet as you go.

  1. 01 · SURVEY — Tape-measure real vigueta centers, chalk all 12 lines + the 9 rafter lines R1–R9 and 4 tie lines of D-01. Pilot-hole the capa, verify 60 mm. Buy 2 sample T-bolts P and check the rail channel (D-06).
  2. 02 · CUT — Cut all steel per the parts index (A–H). Keep the two rows' parts in separate marked piles — B2/C2 look like offcuts.
  3. 03 · WELD — Jig + weld rafter #1 per D-03, verify TOS diagonals, then clone: 9 × type 1, 9 × type 2 (D-04). Weld seat tabs G on the chords. Test coupons before real joints (4 mm fillets). Prime + paint everything.
  4. 04 · ANCHOR — Lay the 4 ties E on their lines; drill Ø12 at every vigueta crossing + the east wall-line pairs (D-07, D-01); clean, inject, set rods J. Full cure → proof-test 3 per tie @ 4 kN. Bed contact points in sealant V, torque down.
  5. 05 · ERECT — 10° row first: set rafters on R1–R9, base ends on ties, plumb, square by diagonals (±5 mm / 9.14 m), fillet-weld bases to ties, cold-galv the welds. Repeat 30° row. Weld X-braces F1/F2 in bays R2–R3 and R7–R8 (D-05).
  6. 06 · RAIL — String-line each seat plane to ±3 mm, shim on tabs only. Set rails K on pads Q, T-bolts P finger-tight; splices L at the D-01 stations; verify 9300 overall + 80 cantilevers; torque.
  7. 07 · PANEL — South row first, west→east. Two people. Leap-frog connect each panel before setting its neighbor. Clamps M/N per D-08: 12–16 N·m, 10–12 overlap, clear of drain holes.
  8. 08 · GROUND — Everything in D-09: U at each frame, lugs S, jumpers T, continuity < 1 Ω. Then the wiring plan (Rev. B) takes over.
Hold points (do not pass): anchor cure + proof test before any structure load · 35 mm mid clamps do not grip these 30 mm frames · nothing bears on bovedilla · never step on modules.
